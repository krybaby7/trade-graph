What each package built:
- **W11-A (D285–D289).** One protected kernel function
  (`kernel/market_type.py`) labels a coin at a moment from completed hourly
  candles only, using TA-Lib's average, ADX, +DI and −DI (rules
  `market-type-rules@1`: a 168-hour average and its value 24 hours earlier,
  ADX 14 with a trend at 20 or more). Live labels and replays call the same
  function. The hourly scan labels the shortlist and the day's coins
  (focus, held, planned, alarmed) and keeps the labels in a new table
  (migration 0050); `trade-graph market-types` prints the newest ones. A
  strategy may require a market type at entry (`is` / `is_not`), and every
  new practice report splits its trades by the market type at entry, summing
  exactly to the report's totals. Old strategies, reports and their hashes
  are unchanged.
- **W11-B (D290–D294).** The desk's wake reply may carry `lab_requests`
  (register a strategy version, create a study, tune, confirm), with no cap
  (D116). A lab worker in the installed service runs them one at a time in
  its own thread with its own read-only candle handle, so it never delays
  trading or protection; it never places or cancels an order; an
  interrupted request runs again and is never half recorded. When a batch is
  done, the desk is woken with the new `lab_results` reason, and its `lab`
  context shows the newest results in plain sentences, what is queued, the
  tries per strategy family and the newest untouched confirmation window
  (walk-forward). Refused requests come back with their reason. On first
  start an installation registers the two example strategies; the desk asks
  for their confirmations itself. The practice range and the lab are now
  protected paths, so the deployed Engineer cannot change the fair-test
  rules. Migration 0051 adds the lab's queue tables and the new wake reason.

What it built (D295–D299):
- **Playbook (D295).** A desk reply may give a whole new playbook revision:
  per market type a stance, the strategy versions it means to use (none for
  "wait"), a reason and the report ids it rests on. Revisions are stored
  append-only with who, when, why and the revision they replaced, and can be
  read at any past moment. It is a record, not a rule.
- **Stances and ideas for the desk (D296).** Per coin it considered, the
  desk records wait, buy dips, follow the trend or other, the market type it
  saw, the strategy version, the news it weighed and a one-sentence reason.
  The Leader may now address a strategy idea to the desk; it wakes the desk
  and waits seven days in its lab inbox. Software never queues a lab run
  from an idea; the desk decides what to test.
- **What a desk wake shows (D297).** Each held, planned, focus, trigger and
  shortlist coin carries its market-type label, marked current, stale or
  missing, never guessed. New context keys `playbook` and `news` (the newest
  Leader round with the desk's note and ideas, and Jev's recent verdicts and
  would-be alarms, shadow mode included). A last step trims the desk's own
  parts in a fixed order, counting what it leaves out, so every request
  stays under 128 KiB (the busy test day is now 99,604 bytes).
- **Each day plan records its market type and playbook revision (D298),**
  inside the installation; it never refuses a plan (D280 item 5, tested: a
  plan the playbook says to skip, on a falling coin, still installs).
- **Protected paths (D299):** the desk's contract and records are protected
  from the deployed Engineer. Migration 0052 adds the playbook, stance and
  day-plan record tables and lets a strategy idea go to the desk.
