What it does: it puts the owner's D138 limits into the policy. The daily loss
stop resets at the day start (00:00 UTC). The per-coin and total exposure
limits are checked as if every entry filled at the worst of its price, the
quote and the mark, after fees. Only coins that passed the scanner can be
bought; a stale scan refuses new entries and never blocks exits. No-riba
refusals cover futures, perpetuals, staking or earn symbols and leverage. The
command `trade-graph money-limits` previews by default and records only with
`--confirm`. Day-plan entries are refused until the owner records limits at
step L3. Property tests over random fill sequences found one real gap, now
closed: a buy resting below the market could fill past the per-coin share
under the old valuation.

Second run, 71 modules: 1,553 passed. `tests/unit`: 2,138 passed, 7 skipped. |

What it does: the Trader writes one plan per focus coin, with resting buy
entries below the ask and a stop exit, plus an optional take-profit. A plan
and all its order intents are saved in one transaction before anything is
sent. A replaced plan first cancels and reconciles the old entries, and is
refused while any of them has an unknown status. A safety exit is attached in
the same transaction that records each fill, partial fills included. Any pause
cancels resting entries and keeps the exits. Plans need a practice-range
confirmation report (no pass mark) and the owner's recorded money limits. No
confidence threshold, committee or Leader approval exists. W4-A also fixed,
at its root, the pre-existing partial-fill rounding bug that wave 3 found
(D189).

Integration (fresh subagent): no conflicts. Merge `2b6a4f7`; shared-file commit

What they do:
- **W5-A event engine:** typed wake, check-in and focus-step contracts in
  `contracts/wakes.py`. Departments wake on price and indicator alarms, entry
  fills, safety-exit fills, the daily loss stop, the check-ins they choose and
  wake requests. Later triggers batch into the open wake and are never
  dropped; nothing caps wakes. The daily cycle runs the scan, then Research
  focus, then the Trader's day plans, then the Learning review at day end. It
  is opt-in and off by default.
- **W5-C scorecard:** `trade-graph scorecard` reports the result after fees
  against holding cash and holding BTC, in EUR. It reuses W2-A's sums. AI
  spending for the same days comes from receipts, and unknown stays unknown.
  It states that working software, economic evidence and permission for real
  money are separate gates.
- **W5-B charts:** Lightweight Charts 5.2.1 (Apache-2.0), vendored from the npm
  registry with its integrity checked. HQ's Money tab shows candles,
  indicators, alarm lines and orders, all from saved records. They come from
  a separate `GET /api/v1/hq/chart`, so the HQ snapshot does not grow. HQ's
  own TradingView attribution sits under every chart. Nothing is fetched from
  outside at runtime.
