## Wave 3: money limits and coin list

| Package | Task | Branch (head) | Pull request | Merge commit | Package tests (no full suite per package) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| W3-A | T32 money limits | `claude/t32-money-limits` (`53fc001`) | [#10](https://github.com/krybaby7/trade-graph/pull/10) | `ba14f1a` | 6 new files: 147 passed. Related files in four runs: 2,147 passed. After the fix: 113 related modules, 2,832 passed, 0 failed, 14 skipped (bwrap). |

What it does: it puts the owner's D138 limits into the policy. The daily loss
stop resets at the day start (00:00 UTC). The per-coin and total exposure
limits are checked as if every entry filled at the worst of its price, the
quote and the mark, after fees. Only coins that passed the scanner can be
bought; a stale scan refuses new entries and never blocks exits. No-riba
refusals cover futures, perpetuals, staking or earn symbols and leverage. The
command `trade-graph money-limits` previews by default and records only with
`--confirm`. Day-plan entries are refused until the owner records limits at
step L3. Property tests over random fill sequences found one real gap, now
closed: a buy resting below the market could fill past the per-coin share
under the old valuation.

Full suite, first candidate `290713e`: GitHub job 117269384964 had 2 failed,
7,080 passed and 18 skipped. Both failures were
