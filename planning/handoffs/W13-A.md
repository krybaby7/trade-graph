# W13-A C13: The Trading desk room and the scorecard by strategy

For the owner: HQ's home tab now has a Trading desk room below the map that shows, in plain words, the desk's playbook
(for rising, sideways, falling and unclear markets: which tested strategy it uses or that it waits in cash, and why),
today's choice for each coin with its market type, what its strategy lab is testing and found (each result after fees
next to simply holding the coin), the Leader news it read, and what each strategy earned or lost in each kind of
market. The scorecard now splits every trade by the strategy version, the market type when it bought and the desk's
stance, with the same totals, and the Learning department reads that split so its lessons can say which choice earned
or lost where. It was checked only with made-up prices and a scripted desk in a test browser, so after the install
please open the HQ tab once on your phone and iPad, and at the install (checklist step 3) add the word `scorecard` to
Learning's list in your subscription profile.

Branch: `claude/c13-desk-hq-scorecard`, head: the commit that adds this handoff's pull-request link (code, tests and
docs at `14808c0`; the handoff first committed in `fc6d51e`). Pull request: https://github.com/krybaby7/trade-graph/pull/30 (into `claude/day-trader-engine-20261012`, not merged). Based on `e006d07`

## Decisions (reserved block D300–D304)

- **D300 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C13): the scorecard's split.** Each trade and each position still
  held counts once, by the choice behind its first buy: the strategy version of the day plan whose waiting buy filled
  (`no_day_plan` when none placed it), the market type software labelled the coin with at that fill (the newest label
  made by then; `stale` when it was more than 2 hours old, D287; `missing` when there was none; never a guess) and the
  desk's stance recorded with that day plan (D298; `not_recorded` when none). Coins held at the period's start count as
  `carried_in` in all three. A position still held counts with its value as if sold at the end with the taker fee
  (the scorecard's own end valuation), so every list (`by_strategy`, `by_market_type`, `by_stance` and the
  combinations `by_choice`) sums exactly to the trades, wins, losses, even trades, open positions and result after
  fees (`kernel/trade_metrics.split_results`, unchanged); whatever no trade explains is `outside_trades` (zero for
  paper fills, tested). Market type at entry is the label at the fill, not at the plan's installation, because a
  resting buy can fill hours later (the plan's record keeps the installation label). The split is additive to the
  scorecard document (`split`, with `entries` per trade); a record that can't be read (an edited, hash-checked label)
  makes only the split unavailable with the reason, never the scorecard. Reason: the package text ("by strategy
  version, market type at entry and stance, with the same sums"), the night lead's "use as_of=<fill time>", D289's
  split rule for the practice range, AGENTS.md (unknown is never zero, point-in-time inputs).
- **D301 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C13): Learning reads the split.** A Learning turn's context gains
  `scorecard`: the paper scorecard since the account opened (period, status, result), the split's totals and the rows
  that hold a trade or a position still held, at most 24 strategy versions and 24 combinations and the 12 newest
  trades (the rest counted, never a cap on trading or learning, D116), with a note that it is evidence, not a pass mark;
  an account that can't be scored says why. The key joins Learning's protected allowlist (`ROLE_CONTEXT_KEYS`); like
  W12-A's keys, the owner's pinned subscription profile must list it (L3 step 3), and without it Learning answers as
  before. Reason: the package ("Learning can read the split, so lessons can say which choice earned or lost in which
  market"), W12-A's D297 precedent.
- **D302 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C13): the Trading desk room is its own read.** `GET /api/v1/hq/desk`
  (`trade-graph.hq.desk.v1`), never inside the snapshot: the busy week with W8-C's and W10-C's fullest blocks is
  already about 75 KB of the snapshot's 80 KB (W10-C), as the charts, wake log and day plans are separate (D197, D215,
  D251). The panel sits on the HQ tab below the map (the map's Trader room is already called "Trading desk"; its sheet
  opens the room), in the left column on a desktop, and reads only while on screen, again with HQ's 30-second poll. It
  shows five parts, each `shown`, `empty`, `not_available` (an older database or a live account's scorecard) or
  `unreadable` (said as such; the others still show): the playbook in force per market type with reasons and the
  revision it replaced; today's newest stance per coin with the label the desk saw and the label now; the lab as the
  desk sees it (`lab_context`) with each shown report's trades and result after fees against holding the coin (from
  the stored report's `hold_coin` benchmark); the news at the desk's newest turn (`news_context` at that task's moment
  for its coins: round, inbox, Jev); and the scorecard's split from HQ's 60-second trading cache. Page bounds: 16
  coins, 8 results with 4 reports, 8 families, 5 headlines, 3 ideas, 5 inbox ideas, 5 verdicts, 8 rows per split list,
  each with its count of the rest. The busiest synthetic room is 62,699 bytes (compact JSON, as served); the scripted
  week's 11,508. Reason: the package text, D280, HQ-DASHBOARD's budget and "reading starts nothing".
- **D303 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C13): the lab in HQ's problems and Story (W11-B's open items).** The
  service's `strategy_lab:<error>` failure has words of its own ("the strategy lab's last step failed"; the desk's lab
  requests wait; trading, protection and records are unaffected; no "practice", so a live account reads them too). The
  Story lists the lab's finished requests from its `strategy_lab` activities under the Trading desk: requests ending
  within 15 minutes of each other are one item with their count and outcomes (a busy lab never floods the 60-item
  page), a failed request needs a look, a refused one is neutral (the fair-test rules at work), a lab that couldn't
  start (`not_ready`) needs a look; starts and requests put back by a stop are not results. Reason: W11-B's handoff,
  D255's pattern for the news check.
- **D304 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C13): no migration and no new rule.** Everything here reads existing
  records (0034, 0050, 0051, 0052) through the packages' own read functions; there is no state migration (0053 stays
  unused) and no candle-database change. The split lives in the protected scorecard; the kernel, the desk's, labels',
  lab's and practice range's packages are unchanged; HQ's new code is a view like the other HQ projections (not
  protected). Nothing here refuses, approves or changes a plan, an order or the playbook (D280 item 5). Reason: the
  package ("prefer none"), AGENTS.md.
