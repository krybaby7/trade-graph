# W12-A C12: One Trading desk: strategy choice, playbook, news

For the owner: The Trader is now the Trading desk: on every wake it sees whether each coin it watches is rising,
sideways, falling or unclear, reads the Leader's news and the news alarm's verdicts, and records per coin whether it
waits in cash, buys dips, follows the trend or does something else, with a one-sentence reason. It keeps a playbook of
which tested strategy it uses in which kind of market, the Leader can now send it strategy ideas that it decides
whether to test in its own lab, and every day plan records the coin's market type and the playbook in force, while
software never refuses a plan for not following the playbook. Everything was tested only with made-up prices, a
scripted Leader and a scripted desk in the cloud; the one thing for you is at the install (checklist step 3): add the
words `playbook` and `news` to the Trader's list in your subscription profile.

## For W13-A: the read functions

All take an `sqlite3.Row` connection (HQ's read-only one works) and give `None` / `[]` / `{}` on a database before
0052. Module `trade_graph.application.trading_desk.store` unless named otherwise.

- **Playbook**: `read_playbook(connection, portfolio_id, *, as_of=None) -> PlaybookRecord | None` (the revision in force
  at a moment: the newest made by then); `read_playbook_revisions(connection, portfolio_id, *, since=None, until=None)`
  (each with `revision`, `previous_revision_id`, `set_by`, `wake_id`, `task_id`, `why`, `choices`, `created_at`;
  hash-checked). `PlaybookRecord.choice_for(market_type)` gives one type's `PlaybookChoice` (`stance`,
  `strategy_refs`, `reason`, `report_ids`).
- **Stances**: `read_stances(connection, portfolio_id, *, since=None, until=None, symbols=None, wake_id=None)` and
  `read_latest_stances(connection, portfolio_id, *, as_of=None, since=None) -> {symbol: StanceRecord}`. A
  `StanceRecord` has `stance`, `market_type_seen` (what the desk said), `label_status` and `label_market_type` (what
  software showed it), `label_id`, `strategy_ref`, `news_refs`, `reason`, `playbook_revision_id`, `wake_id`, `created_at`.
- **A day plan's market type and playbook revision**: `read_day_plan_desk_records(connection, *, portfolio_id=None,
  plan_ids=None) -> {plan_id: DayPlanDeskRecord}`: `market_type` (newest label at installation) with `label_status`
  (`current` / `stale` / `missing`; use `market_type_in_force`, None unless current), `label_id`, `labelled_at`,
  `playbook_revision_id`, `playbook_revision`, `stance_id`, `stance`, `recorded_at`. A plan installed before 0052 has
  no record. For "market type at entry" of a fill, `market_types.store.read_latest_market_types(connection,
  symbols=[symbol], as_of=<fill time>)` gives the label then; the plan record gives the label at installation.
- **News the desk read**: `trade_graph.application.trading_desk.news.news_context(connection, portfolio_id, *, coins,
  at, budget=NEWS_CONTEXT_BYTES)` (`leader_round` with summary, headlines, `desk_note`, `strategy_ideas_for_desk`;
  `lab_inbox`; `jev` with `mode`, verdicts and routes). The exact `news` a turn read is in that task's snapshot.
- **Playbook as the desk sees it**: `trade_graph.application.trading_desk.context.playbook_context(connection,
  portfolio_id, *, at)`. Each wake's outcome names `playbook_revision_id` and `stance_ids` (`WakeOutcomeRecord`).
- Through the service's database: `DeskStore(database, clock).playbook(...)`, `.stances(...)`, `.day_plan_record(...)`.

## Decisions (reserved block D295–D299)

- **D295 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C12): the playbook.** A desk answer may carry `playbook`: a whole new
  revision with, per market type it covers (each once), a stance, the strategy versions it means to use (none for
  "wait"), a reason of at most 300 characters and up to 8 report ids it rests on, and why it changed (500). Software
  stores it append-only in `desk_playbook_revisions` with who (`set_by` = trader, SQL-checked), the wake and task,
  when, why, the revision it replaced and its SHA-256-checked document; a portfolio's revisions form one chain
  (revision + 1, naming the newest, never earlier than it; SQL trigger), so the revision in force at any past moment is
  the newest made by then, like lessons. No answer without `playbook` changes it. It is the desk's record, not a rule
  (D280 item 5). Reason: the package text, D280 items 1 and 5, AGENTS.md (point-in-time inputs and revisions).
- **D296 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C12): stances and ideas for the desk.** A desk answer may give one
  stance per coin it considered (up to 32 per answer, an envelope, D116); each is stored in `desk_stances` with the
  label software showed for that coin in the wake (or, for a coin the wake did not show, the label at the wake's
  moment) and the playbook revision in force after the answer. Only the Trader keeps a playbook and gives stances
  (others' are refused with a note). `StrategyIdea.department` gains `trader`: the Leader's guidance sends strategy
  ideas to the desk and cost and quality ideas to Optimisation; an idea for the desk goes to it when software wakes
  it (else Research, as before), wakes it with a note and waits in its lab inbox (7 days, newest first, with the lab
  requests whose note cites its id); software never queues a lab run from an idea. Reason: D280 items 1, 2 and 4, the
  package text ("the desk decides what to test").
- **D297 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C12): what a desk wake shows, within the bound.** Only the Trader's
  wakes show market types: held, planned, focus, trigger and the day's shortlist coins, at most 32, each `current`
  (label at most 2 hours old, D287), `stale` (its word and age, "not known now") or `missing`; an unreadable label
  shows every coin as missing with the reason and the wake goes on; the shortlist's coins carry the word and status.
  New context keys `playbook` (6 KiB: the revision in force, the one before, the count, the newest stance per coin of
  the last 24 hours) and `news` (12 KiB: the newest completed Leader round, the lab inbox, Jev's verdicts of the last
  24 hours on the desk's coins or Kraken with their routes and the switch's mode, shadow included). A last step trims
  the desk's own parts in a fixed order (older news to half, older stances, shortlist-only market types, wake
  triggers to 20, news to a quarter, triggers to 10), counting what it leaves out, until the context fits 128 KiB
  minus the instruction, the reply schema and an 8 KiB margin. Measured: a busy synthetic day without it was
  143,852 bytes (refused by the subscription bound); with it 99,604 bytes and 2,307 JSON nodes (bound 10,000).
  Reason: the package's "within the size limit" and "busy-day test", W6-D's and W11-B's shares.
- **D298 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C12): a day plan records its market type and playbook revision.**
  In the installation transaction (a savepoint) `day_plan_desk_records` keeps the coin's newest label at that moment
  (status, type, label id, when), the playbook revision in force (id and number) and the desk's stance on the coin in
  the same answer (by task id); SQL checks that it names its own plan, coin, revision and stance. It never checks or
  refuses anything (D280 item 5, tested: a plan against the playbook on a falling coin is installed); a record that
  cannot be written leaves the plan installed and the `installed` event says `not recorded`. Reason: the package text
  ("for Learning and the scorecard"), W13-A.
- **D299 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C12): protected paths and the desk's words.** The desk's contract
  and package (records, readings, news and context) are protected paths: what the desk chose in which market type is
  what the scorecard splits by. Migration 0052 is under `adapters/persistence/`. The desk's job is in the code
  (`DESK_WAKE_INSTRUCTION`, reaching every installation) and in the R1 prompt `prompts/trader.md` (a new installation's
  baseline; an installed database keeps its active prompt until the Engineer's normal path changes it). Reason: AGENTS.md
  (protected acceptance and accounting records), W11-B's D294.
