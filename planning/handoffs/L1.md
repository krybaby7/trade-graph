# L1: Keep price data current (done 2026-10-19)

For the owner: Your PC now has six and a half months of Kraken prices: every coin from 1 April at 5, 15 and 60
minutes, and Bitcoin and Ether without a break from 1 April to 19 October at 1, 5, 15 and 60 minutes. The two Windows
price tasks are registered and ran at your next login, and three separate checks show the archive, the collector and
the rebuild from Kraken's trade history agree candle for candle. Nothing is missing for L2, and you need to do
nothing except stay logged in to Windows so the price tasks can run.

Branch: `claude/day-trader-engine-20261012` (local commits, not pushed). Local step, no pull request.
Everything here used free public Kraken data and wrote only the private candle file
`~/.local/share/trade-graph-market/market_data.sqlite` (D132). No key, no paid call, no order; the trading system was
not started. Commands ran from the pinned collector checkout `candle-collector-runtime` (`138aa5a`) under the
collection lock. The owner was told before each `trade-graph` command (D263).
