every coin, month range and fee level (59 of 60 results), because before fees they roughly break even and the fees
then decide the result. In the falling months (April to July) they lost less than simply holding the coin; in the
rising months (August and September) they missed almost all of the rise. Before the install you decide whether the
team first works on better strategies; also, the install checklist misses a step without which the Trader could make

## Finding for L3: confirmation reports must exist inside the installed system

`DayPlanService` refuses a day plan unless its strategy version has a practice-range **confirmation** report in the
same state database (`application/day_plans.py`, D138 item 5), and the Trader's `practice_range` context lists only
versions with one (`application/trader_workflow.py`). Only the `practice-range-*` commands create reports; no
department or service does. [L3-INSTALL-CHECKLIST.md](../../docs/L3-INSTALL-CHECKLIST.md) has no step that registers
strategies and runs confirmation replays in `/var/lib/trade-graph/trade_graph.sqlite`. As written, after L3 every day
plan would be refused and the scored test would make no trade. Needed: a checklist step after step 7 (the candle file
is in place) and before step 15, inside the new image: `practice-range-register`, `practice-range-study` and the
confirmation `practice-range-run` for each strategy version the Trader may use, with the strategy files available in
the image or the owner folder. Also: no department can create practice reports, so new strategy versions reach the
Trader only through these owner-run commands.

**Finding: one missing hour blanks a coin's market type for about 36 days.** `market-type-rules@1` needs 863 complete
hourly candles. Kraken's hourly archive has three short holes in the window, identical for every coin (2025-01-25
from 15:00, 3 hours; 2025-08-28 from 09:00, 1 hour; 2025-11-01 from 16:00, 5 hours), so about 108 days came out
`unclear` on Kraken (613 of 3,618 slow-trend-pullback tuning trades), against 11 trades on OKX, whose hourly series
has no hole (only the first days of warm-up). Live, any collector or exchange outage of one hour would leave the desk
without market types for five weeks. Recommended before L3: tolerate short gaps (for example by bridging up to a few
missing hours, or restarting the run-in only over the gap) as a recorded rules revision, with a test on these three
real gaps.

**Fixed on 2026-10-21 (D230, at the owner's request before L3):** `market-type-rules@2`, now the default, skips a hole
of up to 6 missing hours with a candle on both sides, at most 24 skipped hours in a label's 887-slot look-back
([MARKET-TYPES.md](../../docs/MARKET-TYPES.md), "Version 2"); `@1` and its hash are unchanged. Real-data check in a
throwaway practice file (`practice-v2-check.sqlite`): slow trend pullback on Kraken BTC/USD, Nov 2024–Mar 2026, Kraken
after-10k fees, gave the same 482 trades and the same −5,129.51 as before (the strategy does not read market types),
and its split moved from 80 rising / 302 sideways / 18 falling / 82 unclear to 88 / 359 / 35 / **0 unclear**. Tests:
`tests/unit/test_market_type_gap_bridging.py`, `tests/integration/test_market_type_gap_bridging_labels.py` and a
property test with random holes in `tests/property/test_market_type_properties.py`.
