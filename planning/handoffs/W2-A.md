# W2-A T29: Practice range: replay strategies on history with real fees

For the owner: Software can now replay a written-down strategy on months of stored prices, with the Kraken and OKX
fee tables and the same cautious fills as the paper account, and write a report of trades, wins and losses, the
result after fees against keeping cash and against holding Bitcoin, the worst fall and the longest losing run. It
tunes a strategy on earlier days and checks it once on later days that it refuses to tune on, and every report says
plainly that it is evidence for the Trader, not a pass mark, not proof of profit and not permission for real money.
The cloud could not reach Kraken's archive or okx.com, so everything here ran on test data and short recorded Kraken
samples; the first real three-month reports and a real OKX recording are step L2 on your PC, and nothing here needs
a decision from you.

Branch: `claude/t29-practice-range`, head: the commit that adds this line; code at `1e5a9a6`. Pull request:
https://github.com/krybaby7/trade-graph/pull/9.

## What was built

## Decisions (reserved block D165–D169)

- **D165 (2026-10-16, implementation default for T29): strategy versions are a bounded data grammar.** A practice
  strategy is a JSON document validated by `PracticeStrategy`: up to 8 T27 menu indicators with menu-bounded
  settings, up to 8 entry conditions (all must hold) and 8 rule exits comparing a candle field, an indicator output
  or a fixed level with `above`, `below`, `crosses_above` or `crosses_below` (both sides rounded half-even to 12
  significant digits, as alarms compare, D136), a market or resting limit buy (price operand less an offset, a life
  in candles), a mandatory stop (D117), an optional take-profit and holding limit, and an equity fraction of at
  most 1. Intervals 1, 5 and 15 minutes only (D119). It holds no code and cannot choose a side, borrow or short
  (D127); unused indicators and unknown fields are refused. A version is registered once in the state database as
  `strategy_id@version` with the SHA-256 of its canonical document; a change is a new version. The grammar is not
  yet an R1 Engineer artifact class (`artifact_policy.py` is unchanged); adding it there is later work if
  Optimisation or the Engineer should propose versions. Reason: D118 and the AGENTS.md rule that agent-made
  strategies are allowlisted data, while the replay needs rules a machine can apply.
- **D166 (2026-10-16, implementation default for T29): tune earlier, confirm later, never reuse.** A study fixes
  both windows in whole UTC days before any replay. The store and SQL triggers refuse a study that would tune a
  family on days any of its studies set aside for confirmation, or confirm on days the family already tuned or
  confirmed on; a tuning replay stays inside its study's tuning window (sub-windows allowed); a confirmation replays
  the whole confirmation window, and the first version confirmed there spends it, so another version needs a new
  study with later, untouched days. Each confirmation report lists the study's tuning runs and versions so
  selection is visible. D119's "at least three months" is read as 90 replayed days, tuning plus confirming, and
  every report states whether its study meets it (a label, not a refusal: a report is evidence, D138 item 5).
- **D167 (2026-10-16, implementation default for T29): replay semantics.** Candles are read point-in-time at a fixed
  data cut-off (the run's time), so a rerun reads the same revisions; decisions use event time only, with the
  engine's own value at each candle. Per candle: pending market sell at the open, then the protective exit armed
  before the candle, then a market entry at the open or a resting limit during the candle (a new fill re-arms the
  exit on all that is held and checks the same candle, stop only); at the close equity is marked, orders whose life
  ran out are cancelled (lives count candle slots, present or missing; a missing candle fills nothing) and the
  decision is made; no order at the window's last close. One position at a time; entries sized at the taker fee
  plus entry slippage so a fill never needs more cash than there is (otherwise refused, never borrowed); stops and
  targets from the limit price (or the first market fill), rounded against the trader when a tick is given;
  without instrument rules quantities floor to 0.00000001. A position held at the end is valued at the last close
  less exit slippage and the taker fee and is not counted as a trade. Replays use the newest revision of each cost
  profile (the fees a strategy would pay now) and fill policy `bar-replay@1`, both named with their hashes.
  Benchmarks: cash (zero, D127), holding BTC of the same quote currency and source (reported unavailable when there
  are no such candles, never invented) and holding the coin itself, each bought at the window's first open and
  sold at its last close at the taker fee. A report's deterministic part is hashed (`result_sha256`);
  `practice-range-verify` replays it from its own inputs and records whether it was reproduced.
- **D168 (2026-10-16, implementation default for T29): OKX candles.** OKX public candles are their own source,
  `okx_public_candles` (D132), added by candle-database version 0002, which rebuilds the two tables that name sources
  as SQLite documents; every row keeps its id and the append-only triggers return. The parser follows OKX's
  documented `history-candles` format (newest first, nine text fields, `confirm` "0" for the forming candle,
  dropped unseen) and refuses a whole page on anything unexpected. OKX reports no trade count or vwap, so OKX
  candles carry `trade_count` 0 (unknown) and no vwap. A recorded folder is imported at import time under its
  manifest's label (`synthetic_fixture` or `recorded_public_fixture`); a live fetch stores `live_public` and pauses
  0.25 s between requests (OKX documents 20 requests per 2 seconds); a scripted transport is labelled synthetic. Pairs
  quoted in USDT or USDC are replayed and reported in that currency, not converted to USD. okx.com was unreachable
  from the cloud on 2026-10-16, so the format, host (`https://www.okx.com`) and limits are this build's reading of
  the documentation; the only OKX fixture is synthetic and labelled, and L2 records a real one.
- **D169 (2026-10-16, implementation default for T29, contract for T42): one set of result sums.** The sums live in
  the protected kernel (`kernel/trade_metrics.py`). A trade is a round trip from flat to flat on one symbol with
  every partial fill and fee; it wins above zero after fees, loses below, and exactly zero is a flat trade that ends
  a losing streak. Worst drawdown is the largest fall from a running peak of the equity curve as a fraction of that
  peak (equity marked at each candle's close, the final point the end valuation). Fees paid count fills only (an end
  valuation's selling fee is inside the ending equity). Sums are exact; ratios and averages are rounded half-even to
  ten decimal places. Reason: D119's report and the build plan's "the scorecard reuses the practice-range sums".
