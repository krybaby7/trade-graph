# W11-A C10: Market types

For the owner: Once an hour, software now labels each coin the team works with as rising, sideways or falling (or
unclear when it lacks enough recent prices), from the same Kraken price history and with no AI, and you can read the
newest labels with `trade-graph market-types`. Every new practice-range report also shows how many trades a strategy
made, and what it earned or lost after fees, when the coin was rising, sideways, falling or unclear at the moment it
decided to buy, and a strategy may now say it only buys in a given market type; older reports and the two starting
strategies are unchanged. Nothing needs a decision from you: the labels were tested only on made-up prices, so after
the merge the local session labels real coins and reruns the practice range, and the Trading desk starts reading the
labels in wave 12.

Branch: `claude/c10-market-types`, head: the commit that adds this handoff's pull-request link (code, tests and docs
at `5640174`; the handoff first committed in `f02fea7`). Pull request: https://github.com/krybaby7/trade-graph/pull/28
(not merged). Based on `663465e`.

## For W12-A: the read function for labels

`trade_graph.application.market_types.store.read_latest_market_types(connection, *, symbols=None, as_of=None)`
returns `{symbol: StoredMarketType}`, the newest label of each coin at or before `as_of`, on any `sqlite3.Row`
connection (read-only included); `{}` for a database before 0050. `StoredMarketType` has `market_type`, `reason`,
`as_of`, `candle_close`, `lag_bars`, `rules_ref`, `evidence`, `document`, `sentence()` and
`is_fresh(at, max_age=DEFAULT_LABEL_AGE)` (2 hours; a stale label should be shown as such, not as the type now).
Through the service's database: `MarketTypeStore(database, clock).latest(symbols=..., as_of=...)`;
`history(symbol, since=, until=)` gives a coin's labels over time. Labels are written hourly with the scan, so the
newest is normally under an hour old.

## Decisions (reserved block D285–D289)

- **D285 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C10): the rules `market-type-rules@1`.** Hourly candles; the simple
  moving average of the close over 168 candles (one week, each weekday once) and its value 24 candles (one day)
  earlier; ADX, +DI and -DI 14 (Wilder's usual setting); a trend counts at ADX 20 or more; a lag of up to 2 candles
  (like the scanner's 2-hour rule, D170). Rising: ADX ≥ 20, +DI > -DI, close above the average, the average higher
  than a day earlier; falling: the mirror; sideways: anything else (weak trend or disagreeing signs); unclear with a
  reason (`no_recent_candle`, `not_enough_candles`, `gap`, `no_value`, `evaluation_failed`). Bounds: 15 or 60
  minutes, average 20–500, slope 1–168, period 7–50, threshold 10–40, lag 0–6; any change is a new version and hash
  (`d017d69a…`, pinned). Reason: the plan's example (a long average's slope, the price's side of it, ADX for
  strength) with the menu's directional indicators (D118); the hourly series is what the collector keeps for every
  pair and the archive has; 20 rather than 25 because the label asks whether a trend is present at all and three
  other checks must agree; every check is a comparison of library outputs or with a level, no in-house ratio.
- **D286 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C10): one function, point in time, protected.** The kernel
  (`kernel/market_type.py`) decides from plain readings; the application supplies the engine's own values per slot
  (`rolling_latest`, the engine's span: ADX 14 reads 863 hourly candles, D136). The live label and the replay end in
  `slot_verdicts` and `MarketTypeTimeline.at` with the same rules; a slot's verdict reads only the 863 slots ending
  at it, and a label at a moment only candles closed (live: also received) by then, so later candles never change it.
  A missing hour makes the label unclear until 863 complete hours follow it (no carry-forward, D136); a coin needs 863
  complete hours before its first label (on the owner's PC from about 2026-10-24, since the collector's hourly series
  starts 2026-09-18). `application/market_types/` is a protected path like the kernel, so the readings cannot be
  steered either. Reason: the plan's "never a second copy" (like D277) and D118/D136.
- **D287 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C10): live labels and their record.** After each live scan the
  service labels the scan's shortlist and the day's coins (`day_coins`: focus, held, planned, alarmed; a superset of
  the plan's shortlist, held and focus, because a coin with a day plan or an alarm is one the desk watches) at the
  scan's moment from `kraken_public_ohlc`, in one transaction, and notes the round in HQ's activity log. An unreadable
  coin is stored as unclear (`evaluation_failed`, with the error's name) and the others carry on; a labelling failure
  is noted and never stops the scan or the collector. State migration 0050 adds `market_type_labels` (append-only,
  hash-checked on read, SQL checks for the point-in-time rule and the reason). `scan-coins` labels the same way after
  a live scan (without held coins); `trade-graph market-types` reads the newest labels read-only. A label is current
  for 2 hours (`DEFAULT_LABEL_AGE`). About 1.4 KB per label; with 20–30 coins an hour about 1 MB a day (retention is
  the same open item as the hourly scans'). Reason: the plan's live step and D174's record pattern.
- **D288 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C10): the grammar condition.**
  `{"market_type": "<rising|sideways|falling|unclear>", "relation": "is" | "is_not"}` among `entry.all_of` only (exits
  do not read it); `unclear` is a label like the others, so `is_not falling` also holds while unclear (add `is_not
  unclear` to require a known type); two different `is`, or `is` and `is_not` of the same type, are refused as never
  true together; a replay of a strategy that reads the type needs the rules. Documents without the condition
  serialise exactly as before (hashes unchanged, tested). Reason: the plan's example, and a condition that always has
  an answer is easier for the desk to reason about than a hidden "unclear never satisfies" rule.
- **D289 (2026-10-20, implementation default for C10): the report split and older reports.** A trade's market type
  is the label at the decision that placed its entry (what the strategy saw; with the condition, all its trades fall
  in the required type); a position still held at the end counts in its entry's type with its valued result (end
  value after the selling fee + sold − bought − fees), so the rows sum exactly to the report's trades, wins, losses
  and result after fees. The section also gives the decision candles per type and the label candles read; it is part
  of the hashed result, and the request names the rules' reference and hash. A request without rules (every report
  made before W11-A) labels nothing and gives exactly the old document, so `practice-range-verify` still reproduces
  old reports (tested on a stored one); a report naming unknown or changed rules is marked not reproduced with the
  reason. The labels' candle runs join the report's evidence labels. When the source has no 60-minute candles, every
  label is unclear and the report says so. Reason: the plan's "split ... summing to the report's totals" and
  "existing reports and their hashes stay valid".
